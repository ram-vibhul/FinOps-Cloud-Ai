# 0.1.0 — unreleased

The first release of CloudCostRadar.

## Costs
- AWS, Azure and Google Cloud costs from their billing exports, plus Snowflake,
  Databricks, Datadog, MongoDB Atlas and AI model spend.
- An explorer to group and filter costs, saved views, showback and chargeback, unit
  costs, FOCUS exports and a one-page leadership report.

## Savings
- Savings ranked by money, risk and effort, with overlapping ideas counted once and
  results checked against later bills.
- Savings Plans and reservation advice, newer instance types, idle and oversized
  resources, and automatic fixes you can switch on with approval.

## Kubernetes
- A read-only agent: costs per namespace and workload, right-sizing, and help when a
  workload breaks.

## Team and control
- Budgets, early warnings and unusual-increase alerts in the app, by email, Slack,
  Microsoft Teams and webhooks.
- Single sign-on, SCIM, custom roles, temporary access, API keys and an audit log.
