# Alerts and notifications
Get told about what matters, where you already work.

## Your inbox
- The bell shows notifications for everything you can see: anomalies, budgets, Radar changes, policies, fixes waiting for approval and more.
- The **Notifications** page (from the bell) chooses, per kind, whether it shows in your inbox and whether it is also emailed to you.

## Slack and Microsoft Teams
- **Settings → Notifications** (administrators) adds a Slack or Teams incoming webhook and chooses which kinds go to it.
- Messages carry buttons that open the right page in CloudCostRadar.

## Webhooks
- Outgoing webhooks send JSON signed with your secret (HMAC-SHA256) for the kinds you choose, with retries and a delivery log.

## Weekly digest and reports
- A weekly digest summarises spend and changes. **Governance → Reports** emails scheduled reports, and the Executive page has a downloadable PDF for leadership, including estimated carbon.
