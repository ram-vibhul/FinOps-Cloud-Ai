# Legal drafts (B.10) — NOT FOR PUBLICATION

These are working drafts written to give a lawyer a complete starting point. **None of
them has been reviewed by a lawyer** (tracker Q6). Do not publish them, link them from the
website or the product, or rely on them until they have been reviewed and the
placeholders are filled in.

| File | What it is | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| `terms.md` | Terms of Service | Hosted CloudCostRadar (SaaS) |
| `eula.md` | End-User Licence Agreement | Self-hosted CloudCostRadar |
| `privacy.md` | Privacy Notice | Website, SaaS and Yugiverse as a business |
| `dpa.md` | Data Processing Agreement | Customer data processed in the SaaS |
| `sla.md` | Service Level Agreement | Paid SaaS plans |
| `refund.md` | Refund and Cancellation Policy | Paid plans and licences |

Placeholders are in **[SQUARE BRACKETS]**. They need decisions from the owner:

- Legal entity, registered/business address, GSTIN, contact emails (legal, support,
  security, privacy/grievance officer) — Q6.
- Governing law and courts (suggested: India, courts at [Chennai], Tamil Nadu) — Q6.
- Plans, prices, currencies, trial length — Q2. Payment providers — Q3.
- Hosting provider and regions, email provider (subprocessors) — Q4, Q7.
- SLA targets and service credits — Q6.

Points for the lawyer to check in particular:

1. Roles under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and the GDPR:
   Yugiverse as Data Processor/processor for customer data, and Data
   Fiduciary/controller for account and website data.
2. Cross-border transfer wording (DPDP Act section 16; GDPR Chapter V and Standard
   Contractual Clauses for EU customers).
3. Limitation of liability and indemnity caps; consumer-protection limits for
   individual (non-business) customers.
4. RBI e-mandate rules for recurring card/UPI payments (B.17) and GST invoicing (B.15).
5. Export controls and sanctions wording.
6. The EULA's restrictions against the proprietary LICENSE file in the repository.
