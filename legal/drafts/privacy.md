## 1. Our roles

- **Account and website data** (who signs up, signs in, contacts us or pays): we decide
  why and how it is processed. We are the *Data Fiduciary* under India's Digital Personal
  Data Protection Act, 2023 ("**DPDP Act**") and the *controller* under the GDPR where it
  applies.
- **Customer Data** in an organisation (cost records, resource names, tags, the people a
  customer invites): we process it on the customer's behalf under the Data Processing
  Agreement. The customer is the Data Fiduciary/controller; questions about it should go
  to that customer first.
- **Self-hosted CloudCostRadar** runs in the customer's environment. We receive none of its data.
