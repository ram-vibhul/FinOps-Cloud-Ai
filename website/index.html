What it does

All your costs together

AWS, Azure and Google Cloud billing exports, Kubernetes costs per namespace and workload, Snowflake, Databricks, Datadog, MongoDB Atlas and AI model spend, in the currency your finance team uses.

Savings you can act on

Idle and oversized resources, Savings Plans and reservations, newer instance types, storage and data transfer. Overlapping ideas are counted once, and every saving is checked against the next bill.

Budgets and early warnings

Budgets per team, project or account, forecasts, unusual increases spotted within a day, and alerts in the app, by email, in Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Kubernetes, properly

A small read-only agent in each cluster: cost per namespace and workload, right-sizing from real usage, and help when a workload breaks.

For finance and leadership

Showback and chargeback, unit costs, a one-page leadership report, FOCUS exports and a glossary that explains every term.

Safe by design

Read-only access to your clouds. Nothing is ever changed unless you switch on automatic fixes yourself, and even then only with approval.

Run it your way

Hosted by us

Sign up and connect a cloud account in minutes. Your first savings show while the first import runs.

In your own environment

Run the same product on your own servers with Docker and PostgreSQL. Data never leaves your network.
