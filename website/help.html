Help

Guides to getting the most out of CloudCostRadar.

  • Connect your clouds — Bring in your AWS, Azure and Google Cloud bills with read-only access.
  • Live Radar — See what each infrastructure change costs minutes after it is made, before the bill shows it.
  • Savings and automatic fixes — Find waste, plan what to do first, and let CloudCostRadar make the safe changes for you.
  • Budgets and policies — Keep spend within limits and catch costly changes before and as they happen.
  • Explore costs and build dashboards — Slice spend any way you need, save the views you use, and put them on your own dashboards.
  • Alerts and notifications — Get told about what matters, where you already work.
  • Kubernetes — See what each namespace, team and workload costs, and right-size them safely.
