Help

Explore costs and build dashboards

Slice spend any way you need, save the views you use, and put them on your own dashboards.

Cost Explorer

  • Costs → Explore: group by service, account, region, team, project, resource, usage type, any tag or virtual tag, with filters and daily, monthly or total views.
  • Virtual tags fix messy or missing tags with rules, without changing anything in your clouds.
  • Change the display currency at the top of the page; exchange rates are daily, and you can set your own.

Saved views and dashboards

  • Save any explorer view, for yourself or for the whole organisation.
  • Dashboards puts saved views side by side as bars, lines or tables. Ranges like "last 30 days" move with the date.

Number format

  • Settings → Your account → Number format writes amounts the way you read them, including Indian grouping in lakhs and crores (12,34,567).
